## Indicator and Warning Matrix

### Scenario
Within any rolling 24-month window, an unidentified actor or structural mechanism will produce anomalous attrition — deaths under contested circumstances or disappearances without communication — among cleared personnel at US defense-aerospace and nuclear-weapons-complex facilities who hold knowledge of the most sensitive compartmented programs, at a rate and pattern distinguishable from baseline workforce attrition.

Time horizon: Continuous rolling monitoring; retrospective validation window 2021-2026; prospective detection ongoing
Actor: Unknown — scenario is actor-agnostic by design; the matrix detects the pattern, not the perpetrator
Target: Cleared scientific, engineering, and intelligence personnel at defense-aerospace and nuclear-weapons-complex institutions, with elevated risk for those at the disclosure boundary (holding both technical knowledge and demonstrated disposition toward oversight testimony or public disclosure)
Objective: Detection of anomalous attrition pattern to enable protective intervention, counterintelligence triage, and oversight notification

### Stages

This scenario does not follow a conventional preparation-positioning-execution-consolidation arc because the actor is unidentified and may not be a unitary agent. Instead, stages are structured around the observable lifecycle of each attrition event and the aggregate pattern:

1. **Precursor environment** — conditions that historically precede or accompany attrition events
2. **Individual-level warning** — observables at the level of a single person before an event
3. **Event** — the death or disappearance itself
4. **Post-event pattern recognition** — aggregate observables that distinguish signal from noise

### Baseline

The United States employs approximately 130,000 personnel with Q or L clearances (DOE) and several hundred thousand with DoD TS/SCI access. Baseline attrition includes natural death, voluntary separation, retirement, and a background rate of suicide, accidental death, and missing-persons cases. Published data indicate the general-population suicide rate among working-age adults is approximately 14 per 100,000 per year. Homicide victimization in the general population runs approximately 6 per 100,000. Missing-persons reports for adults over 40 resolve at rates above 95% within 30 days.

For the specific population of interest — cleared scientific and engineering personnel at national laboratories and research facilities — the expected rate of violent death and unresolved disappearance is lower than the general population due to demographic factors (higher education, higher income, stable employment). Any sustained deviation above this already-low baseline in a bounded geography and institutional set is analytically significant.

### Aggregation Rules

- **YELLOW (Watch):** Any single confirming indicator trips at a facility or among personnel associated with compartmented aerospace/nuclear programs. Initiate enhanced monitoring of the affected facility and program. Specifically: any new pre-emptive exculpatory statement by cleared personnel; any new testimony scheduling for compartmented-program witnesses; any new threat/harassment report from cleared personnel at a facility with a prior event.

- **ORANGE (Elevated):** Two or more independent confirming indicators trip within a 12-month window, OR any single pre-testimony death or disappearance with the essentials-absent signature occurs. Notify counterintelligence and congressional oversight liaisons. Initiate proactive protective measures for identified at-risk personnel. Specifically: any death within 60 days of scheduled testimony triggers ORANGE immediately; any essentials-absent disappearance at a facility with a prior event within 24 months triggers ORANGE.

- **RED (Warning Issued):** Three or more independent confirming indicators trip within a 12-month window, OR geographic/institutional clustering threshold is met (3+ events at same node within 24 months), OR temporal acceleration threshold is met (event-rate doubling between successive 12-month periods). Formal warning product issued to personnel-security leadership, CI leadership, and congressional oversight. Protective-detail assessment for identified at-risk personnel.

- **De-escalation:** Any disconfirming indicator at High reliability downgrades the alert level by one step. Multiple disconfirming indicators at High reliability return to GREEN. The base-rate disconfirming indicator, if it can be performed with the actual classified baseline, is the most powerful de-escalation trigger.

### Notes on Deception

**High-fake-risk indicators:**
- Random-stranger homicide: a staged random crime is established tradecraft; the absence of motive and victim-perpetrator connection, while genuinely unusual, is precisely what a sophisticated staged operation would produce. The Grillmair case — identified suspect, no connection, no motive — sits in the zone of maximum ambiguity between random crime and staged elimination.
- Accidental overdose: polypharmacy deaths can be manufactured with access to the target's environment; the Sullivan case — four substances, none individually lethal at reported levels — is consistent with both accidental combination and sophisticated incapacitation.
- Voluntary disappearance: the essentials-absent signature (departed without phone, wallet, keys) is consistent with both voluntary crisis-driven departure and coerced removal designed to mimic it.

**Hard-to-fake indicators:**
- Pre-emptive exculpatory statements: require the individual's own action, independently witnessed and documented; cannot be manufactured by an external actor.
- Pre-testimony timing: congressional hearing schedules and legal docket dates are institutional records; the coincidence of death with scheduled testimony is independently verifiable and not within an actor's control to create after the fact (only the death is within their control, not the schedule).
- Geographic and institutional clustering: producing multiple events at specific facilities requires operational access to those locations; the pattern itself is hard to fake because it emerges from aggregate data that no single actor controls the collection of.

**Recommended counter-deception steps:**
- For each future event, immediately preserve all digital devices and communication records before they are returned to employers or families.
- For apparent suicides and accidental deaths among cleared personnel, mandate independent forensic review (not facility-local or employer-affiliated pathology) within 48 hours.
- For disappearances with the essentials-absent signature, immediately check whether personal devices were powered down or destroyed (versus simply left behind), and whether the individual's facility access credentials were used after the reported departure time.
- Maintain a running comparison of the observed pattern against the deception-consistent profile: if events consistently present as the hardest-to-investigate categories (suicide, accidental OD, random stranger crime, voluntary disappearance), that consistency is itself a deception indicator.

### Review Cadence

- **Indicators reviewed:** Monthly, or immediately upon any new event matching the indicator profile
- **Threshold adjustment:** Quarterly, incorporating any new information from the federal investigation and any resolved cases
- **Baseline refresh:** Annually, incorporating updated actuarial data if declassified, and any new cross-era analogue cases
- **Matrix retirement:** When the federal investigation produces a final public report with definitive findings, the matrix should be revised against those findings — confirmed indicators are promoted, disconfirmed indicators are retired, and thresholds are recalibrated
