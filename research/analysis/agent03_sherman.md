## 1. DAN SHERMAN BIOGRAPHY

### Verified Facts (Corroborated by Multiple Independent Sources)

- **Full Name:** Dan Sherman (sometimes referenced as "Sergeant Dan Sherman" or "Staff Sergeant Dan Sherman")
- **Military Branch:** United States Air Force (USAF)
- **Service Duration:** Approximately 12 years (enlisted ~1982, separated mid-1990s)
- **Rank at Separation:** Variously reported as Staff Sergeant (SSgt) and Technical Sergeant (TSgt). The most common references say "Staff Sergeant," though some recent sources (2025 Michels interview context) say "Technical Sergeant" or "retired sergeant."
- **Military Specialty:** Electronic Intelligence (ELINT) Specialist -- assigned to the National Security Agency (NSA)
- **Duty Station:** NSA Headquarters, Fort Meade, Maryland (reported assignment ~1992)
- **Awards/Decorations Claimed:**
  - Air Force Commendation Medal
  - Air Force Achievement Medal
  - Recognized for heroism during service (details unspecified)
- **DD214 Status:** Jesse Michels (American Alchemy podcast, October 2025) claims to have obtained and reviewed Sherman's full DD214, service records, and history as an Electronics Intelligence Specialist. The DD214 has been referenced publicly but not independently published in full by a neutral third party for verification.
- **Post-Military Career:** Author of fiction novels. Multiple sources state he has authored "14 internationally published novels" in addition to "Above Black." He also maintained a website at aboveblack.com (later spiritone.com/~ppd/).
- **Current Status:** Alive as of October 2025 (appeared in 3-hour 39-minute podcast interview with Jesse Michels).

### Unverified / Self-Reported Claims

- That he held clearances "above Top Secret" for participation in Project Preserve Destiny
- That his ELINT training at NSA served as "cover" for PPD training
- That he was genetically modified in utero to have latent telepathic abilities
- That he communicated telepathically with extraterrestrial entities
- Specific rank discrepancies between sources (SSgt vs. TSgt) remain unresolved

### Assessment of Verified Biography

Sherman's basic military service -- USAF enlistment, ELINT specialty, NSA assignment at Fort Meade -- is consistent across all sources and is the type of claim easily falsifiable. No source has produced evidence contradicting his military service claims. The DD214 referenced by Michels, if authentic, would corroborate the ELINT specialty but would not confirm or deny involvement in any classified program. Military service records do not typically reference SAP (Special Access Program) assignments.

## 4. CLAIMS ABOUT FUTURE EVENTS / CATASTROPHE

### The Core Prediction

Sherman's central claim regarding future events, as stated across all sources:

**Claim:** There will be "some sort of event in the future that will wipe out all electromagnetic energy" on a worldwide scale. Project Preserve Destiny was created to prepare a network of "intuitive communicators" who could maintain high-level government/military communications after conventional electromagnetic communications fail.

**Source consistency:** This claim is present in:
- The 1997/1998 book "Above Black" (original publication)
- The 2007 Project Camelot interview (Portland, Oregon, June 2007)
- The 2025 American Alchemy interview with Jesse Michels
- Multiple secondary source summaries

**Specificity of the prediction:**
- Sherman does NOT specify the cause of the electromagnetic catastrophe in the book
- He states he was "not privy to the program's full objectives" due to compartmentalization (the "onion" structure)
- In later interviews (post-publication), he has speculated about possible causes including:
  - Environmental collapse
  - Electromagnetic disruptions (possibly solar)
  - "An alien-driven agenda"
- One source references "a potential electromagnetic catastrophe around 2012" but this appears to be a secondary interpretation, not a direct Sherman quote from the book
- Sherman has explicitly stated he "lacks specifics due to his limited clearance"

### The "Containment Scenarios" Claim

**CRITICAL SOURCING NOTE:** Multiple secondary sources (particularly OddWoo and DukeReportBooks) attribute to Sherman claims about receiving "holographic thought-images" that included "Earth's containment scenarios (asteroid deflection, pole shift mitigation)" and "hybrid nurseries, council meetings on crystalline planets." These specific phrases appear to originate from secondary/interpretive sources rather than directly from the 1997 book text. The original book is 147 pages and does not appear to contain these exact phrases based on available excerpts and contemporary reviews.

**Assessment:** The "containment scenarios" including "asteroid deflection" appear to be LATER ADDITIONS to the Sherman narrative, possibly from:
1. Post-publication interviews where Sherman elaborated beyond the book
2. Third-party interpretive summaries that extrapolated from the EM catastrophe claim
3. Community synthesis that merged Sherman's claims with other UFO narrative threads

**This distinction is critically important for any retrodiction testing.**

## 6. CLAIMS ABOUT GOVERNMENT PROGRAMS

### Project Preserve Destiny (PPD)

- **Alleged inception:** 1960
- **Alleged agencies involved:** USAF, NSA
- **Alleged purpose:** Genetic management of humans to produce intuitive communicators; preparation for post-EM-catastrophe communications
- **Classification level claimed:** Above Top Secret; "grey" program (hidden within "black" cover programs)
- **FOIA Status:**
  - Noah Hradek filed FOIA with USAF (July 27, 2023)
  - USAF denied records, forwarded request to NSA (unprompted)
  - NSA issued Glomar response: neither confirmed nor denied under Executive Order 13526
  - Skeptics (John Schindler, former NSA analyst) call FOIA response "meaningless" -- standard bureaucratic procedure, not confirmation
  - Supporters interpret the Air Force-to-NSA forwarding as tacit confirmation of NSA jurisdiction

### "Grey" vs. "Black" Classification

Sherman's terminology distinguishes:
- **Black programs:** Known to exist but classified (e.g., stealth aircraft programs)
- **Grey programs:** Hidden WITHIN black programs; their existence is denied; "above black"
- This terminology is not standard in official classification systems but has some resonance with known SAP/USAP (Unacknowledged Special Access Program) structures

### NSA ELINT Training (Cover Program)

- Sherman's daytime ELINT training at NSA is consistent with known NSA operations and training programs at Fort Meade
- The claim that ELINT training served as "cover" for PPD training is plausible in the SAP context but unverifiable

### NCT (Noise Cancellation Technologies) Connection

- **Claim:** Sherman followed a van from his training facility to NCT in Linthicum, Maryland
- **Verification:** NCT was a real company; Jesse Michels' team verified that DARPA had contracts with NCT in the early 1990s, which aligns with the timeframe Sherman describes
- **Significance:** This is one of the few externally verifiable details in Sherman's account. However, the existence of NCT and its DARPA contracts does not confirm Sherman's claims about the nature of his involvement
- **Counter-argument:** Someone with knowledge of Fort Meade-area defense contractors could incorporate real companies into a fictional narrative

### The Falsifiability Problem

Sherman's claims are structured in a way that makes them extremely difficult to falsify:
- The program is allegedly classified above Top Secret, so no records would be available
- He claims to have no physical evidence
- The communications were subjective (telepathic), so cannot be independently verified
- The prediction (EM catastrophe) has no specific date, so cannot be tested until it either occurs or enough time passes to rule it out
- The genetic modification claim involves technology allegedly not understood by human science

This unfalsifiability is both consistent with a genuine classified program AND consistent with a fabricated narrative designed to be impossible to disprove.

## 12. ASSESSMENT AND CONFIDENCE

### Overall Assessment

Dan Sherman's "Above Black" account occupies a distinctive position in the UFO/UAP literature:

**What CAN be verified:**
- Sherman's military service in the USAF (~1982-mid 1990s): HIGH CONFIDENCE that this is genuine
- His ELINT specialty and NSA assignment: HIGH CONFIDENCE
- The existence of NCT in Linthicum, MD and its DARPA contracts: CONFIRMED
- His consistent retelling of the narrative over 28+ years: CONFIRMED
- His publication of the book in 1997, prior to modern UAP disclosure movements: CONFIRMED

**What CANNOT be verified:**
- The existence of "Project Preserve Destiny": UNRESOLVABLE with current evidence
- Telepathic communication with extraterrestrial entities: UNVERIFIABLE by any current scientific method
- Genetic modification in utero: UNVERIFIABLE
- The EM catastrophe prediction: UNTESTABLE (no timeframe specified)
- The abduction data content: UNPUBLISHED and UNVERIFIABLE

**What raises concern:**
- Sherman is a professional fiction author, capable of constructing compelling narratives
- No corroborating witnesses from the alleged program in 28+ years
- The narrative is structured to be unfalsifiable
- Association with figures of questionable credibility (Boylan)
- Secondary sources have added claims ("containment scenarios," "asteroid deflection," "hybrid nurseries," "galactic council," "crystalline planets") that may not be in the original 1997 book, creating a "legend accretion" problem

**What supports credibility:**
- Absence of apparent financial motive (no significant enrichment)
- Consistent narrative without escalation over decades (unusual for fabricators, who tend to embellish over time)
- The mundane, procedural tone of the book (noted by multiple reviewers)
- Verified military background in a relevant specialty
- NCT/DARPA verification adds a concrete, checkable detail
- The NSA Glomar response, while not confirmation, is notable (though routine for classified queries)

## 13. UNRESOLVED QUESTIONS

6. **What was Sherman's specific discharge reason?** He claims he resorted to "drastic measures" to leave -- what does his DD214 say?

7. **What fiction novels has Sherman published?** A bibliography of his 14 claimed novels could reveal thematic patterns relevant to assessing the credibility of "Above Black."
