## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This report cross-references two independent analytical threads -- one tracking the "missing scientists" attrition cases (14 individuals) and one tracing the chain of custody for claimed UAP materials -- to determine whether overlapping populations, facilities, programs, or temporal correlations exist that would either (a) strengthen the materials hypothesis by showing that people with materials access died under suspicious circumstances, or (b) weaken it by showing the two populations are fundamentally disconnected.

**Bottom Line:** The two populations are almost entirely disjoint. Of 14 attrition cases, only one (McCasland) has a documented connection to the materials-related network, and that connection is indirect (1-2 degrees of separation, through Tom DeLonge's TTSA orbit). No attrition case involves a person who held custody, conducted analysis, or participated in any identified materials program. The materials thread's key personnel (Puthoff, Davis, Green, Bigelow, Vallee, Kelleher, Nolan, Elizondo, Grusch, Lacatski) are all alive as of September 2026. This finding weakens the hypothesis that the attrition pattern is connected to materials disclosure and instead suggests two independent narrative constructions that have been conflated by amplifiers.

## 4. DETAILED ANALYSIS: THE McCASTLAND CASE

McCasland is the sole attrition case with a non-trivial connection to the materials thread. This merits detailed examination.

### 4.1 Materials-Relevant Connections

**Program Access (POSSIBLE):**
- As Commander of the Air Force Research Laboratory, McCasland oversaw the USAF's most advanced aerospace research programs.
- AFRL's portfolio includes advanced materials, propulsion, and directed energy -- all domains cited in materials claims.
- Wright-Patterson AFB (AFRL's headquarters) is historically associated with UAP lore (Project Blue Book was based there) and with Battelle's alleged Roswell debris analysis.
- However, no specific evidence connects McCasland to any UAP materials program. His AFRL command is a necessary but not sufficient condition for access.

**Network Connection (1-2 DEGREES):**
- Named in WikiLeaks-released Podesta emails (2016): Tom DeLonge wrote to John Podesta about McCasland, claiming he had been "working with him for four months."
- DeLonge claimed McCasland had knowledge of "recovered exotic materials connected to UFO incidents."
- McCasland had at least brief involvement with To The Stars Academy's early formation.
- Connection to Puthoff is indirect: both in TTSA orbit, but no direct McCasland-Puthoff collaboration is documented.
- Some commentators have speculated McCasland was "manipulating DeLonge into developing a UFO cover story for new classified American defense technology of a terrestrial origin."

**Materials Facility (YES):**
- Wright-Patterson AFB: historical nexus of all materials claims dating to the Roswell era.
- AFRL conducts classified materials science research.

**Temporal Correlation (YES):**
- McCasland disappeared February 27, 2026.
- This falls during the period of the Trump administration's UAP disclosure push (PURSUE Task Force announced May 1, 2026; NDA waivers under development).
- However, the FBI investigation has not indicated any connection between his disappearance and his former work.

### 4.2 Assessment of McCasland's Relevance to the Materials Hypothesis

McCasland's case is the only one in the attrition list where a materials connection is plausible rather than fabricated. However, several factors weigh against a strong inference:

1. **DeLonge's claims about McCasland are DeLonge's claims**, not McCasland's. McCasland may have humored or briefed DeLonge without having actual materials knowledge.
2. **McCasland's AFRL command ended years before his disappearance.** At the time he vanished, he was Director of Technology at Applied Technology Associates, a private company.
3. **Investigators have not linked his disappearance to UAP connections.** The Silver Alert (issued for elderly/vulnerable adults), his age (68), and the fact he left with hiking gear suggest investigators are considering multiple explanations.
4. **The absence of evidence is not evidence of absence**, but neither is a plausible connection proof of a causal one.

**Confidence that McCasland's disappearance is connected to materials programs:** LOW. The connection is possible but unsupported by any direct evidence. His case is genuinely unexplained, but the materials angle is one of many possible explanations.

## 8. THE "DISCLOSURE BOUNDARY" HYPOTHESIS

### 8.1 Hypothesis Definition

The hypothesis posits that individuals who cross a threshold of knowledge about UAP materials -- a "disclosure boundary" -- face elevated risk of death or disappearance. If true, we would expect:

- Attrition concentrated among individuals with verifiable materials access
- The materials network's most vocal advocates to face the greatest risk
- Deaths/disappearances timed to disclosure events
- Circumstances suggesting professional rather than personal causes

### 8.2 Test Results

| Prediction | Observed | Result |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Attrition among materials-access individuals | 0 of 18 identified materials personnel in attrition list | **FAILS** |
| Most vocal advocates at greatest risk | Elizondo, Grusch, Nolan, Bigelow -- all alive and active | **FAILS** |
| Deaths timed to disclosure events | Superficial correlations only; dissolve under scrutiny | **FAILS** |
| Professional-cause circumstances | Most cases have solved/ordinary explanations | **FAILS** |
| Attrition concentrated in materials-connected population | 13 of 14 cases have ZERO materials connection | **FAILS** |

### 8.3 Assessment

The "disclosure boundary" hypothesis **fails all five testable predictions**. The people who have most aggressively challenged the secrecy regime around materials claims -- publishing books, testifying under oath, briefing the President, co-founding advocacy organizations, filing IG complaints -- are uniformly alive and active. Meanwhile, the attrition cases overwhelmingly involve individuals with no materials connection whatsoever.

If a suppression operation targeting materials-knowledgeable individuals existed, it would be spectacularly incompetent: it would have missed every single public advocate while targeting asteroid scientists, administrative assistants, HVAC technicians, astrophysicists killed by their neighbors, and fusion scientists killed by former classmates.

## 11. ANALYTICAL JUDGMENTS

### Judgment 1: The attrition pattern does not strengthen the materials hypothesis.

**Confidence: HIGH**

The two populations are almost entirely disjoint. The attrition cases overwhelmingly involve individuals with no connection to materials programs, materials custody chains, or the Puthoff network. The sole partial exception (McCasland) has an indirect connection that has not been linked to his disappearance by investigators. The materials hypothesis requires people with materials knowledge to be targeted; the attrition list is populated almost entirely by people without such knowledge.

### Judgment 2: The materials hypothesis is weakened by the survival of its most exposed advocates.

**Confidence: HIGH**

If a suppression mechanism targeted individuals who crossed a "disclosure boundary" for materials knowledge, the expected victims would be Elizondo (public testimony, bestselling book naming programs), Grusch (IG complaint, congressional testimony, restored clearance), Nolan (published analysis, Sol Foundation), Bigelow (briefed the President), and Puthoff (50+ years of involvement). All are alive, active, and increasingly public. Their survival is the strongest single piece of evidence against a disclosure-linked attrition pattern.

### Judgment 3: The two threads share a common narrative structure but not a common population.

**Confidence: HIGH**

Both threads exhibit the same epistemological pattern: a small number of source nodes generating an apparently broad narrative through definitional expansion and backward construction. The materials thread traces to the Puthoff-Davis-Green-Bigelow-Vallee network (N_eff ~1.0). The attrition thread traces to Daniel Liszt's January 2026 YouTube livestream. The conflation of these threads by media amplifiers in early 2026 created a composite narrative ("scientists are being killed for what they know about UAP materials") that is not supported by the underlying evidence in either thread.

### Judgment 4: McCasland remains the only genuinely uncertain case.

**Confidence: HIGH**

McCasland's disappearance is genuinely unexplained, and he is the only attrition case with both a documented (if limited) UAP connection and a plausible materials-program nexus (AFRL command at Wright-Patterson). However, the absence of evidence linking his disappearance to his former work, and the investigative focus on personal-circumstances explanations (Silver Alert, hiking gear taken), make the materials connection speculative rather than evidenced.

### Judgment 5: No "disclosure boundary" population exists in the data.

**Confidence: HIGH**

The concept of a population that appears in both threads -- people who had materials knowledge and who were subsequently targeted -- is not supported. The set intersection between "attrition cases" and "materials-knowledgeable individuals" contains, at most, one indirect case (McCasland) and two extremely tenuous connections (Eskridge's unverified claim, Mack's distant funding link). This is consistent with chance given the population sizes involved.

## 12. BAYESIAN IMPLICATIONS FOR THE MATERIALS HYPOTHESIS

### 12.1 Prior

Agent M03's assessment places the posterior probability for anomalous materials claims at approximately 10.9% (from the prior investigation), with the materials evidence specifically argued to **decrease** this figure due to:
- Every independently tested sample being terrestrial
- N_eff ~1.0 for anomalous claims
- The interview-publication gap (claims exceed data)

### 12.2 Attrition Cross-Reference Update

The attrition cross-reference provides a VERY WEAK downward update to the materials posterior:

- **If materials claims were true AND a suppression operation existed**: We would expect materials-knowledgeable individuals to appear in attrition data. They do not. This is weak evidence against the conjunction of (materials exist) AND (suppression exists).

- **However**: The absence of attrition among materials personnel is equally consistent with (materials exist) AND (no suppression exists). The attrition data primarily speaks to the suppression hypothesis, not the materials hypothesis itself.

- **Net Bayesian impact**: The cross-reference provides approximately zero information about whether anomalous materials exist, because the attrition cases are disconnected from materials programs. It does provide moderate evidence against the specific claim that "people are being killed for knowing about materials."

### 12.3 Revised Assessment

The materials posterior should remain at approximately 10.9% (or slightly below, per M03's recommendation). The attrition cross-reference does not meaningfully update this estimate in either direction, because the two threads address different populations.
