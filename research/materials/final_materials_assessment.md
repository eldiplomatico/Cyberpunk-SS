## I. BOTTOM LINE UP FRONT (BLUF)

We assess with **moderate confidence** that it is **unlikely** (revised posterior probability: approximately 5-8%) that the US government or its defense contractors currently possess recovered physical materials of verified anomalous or non-human origin.

This represents a downward revision from the prior assessment's ~10.9% posterior. The revision is driven by three categories of new evidence developed across this investigation:

1. **Every independently tested sample has yielded terrestrial results.** No fully independent laboratory analysis has ever confirmed anomalous properties in any claimed UAP-related material. The single most probative test -- Oak Ridge National Laboratory's analysis of the "Art's Parts" bismuth-magnesium sample under AARO oversight -- found a conventional industrial or aerospace alloy. This pattern holds across all 15 identified material samples.

2. **The evidence network constitutes a closed epistemic loop.** Formal network analysis reveals an effective independent source count (N_eff) of approximately 1.0-1.2. The minimum vertex cut is 2 (Puthoff and Elizondo): removing these two individuals severs all evidence paths from alleged sources to congressional oversight. Core network density is 0.87 with clustering coefficient approaching 1.0. No claimed evidence path reaches an independent endpoint without passing through at least one core network member.

3. **Alternative hypotheses fully account for the observed evidence without invoking non-human materials.** The combination of H1 (funding ecosystem / institutional capture), H2 (Cold War legacy misattribution, including the Yankee Blue fabricated briefing program), and H3 (sincere belief amplification) is sufficient to explain all major observations. Each hypothesis makes specific predictions that have been confirmed; the residual anomaly hypothesis (H5) makes predictions that have consistently failed.

**Stabilizing factors preventing further downward revision:** Sustained bipartisan congressional action (UAPDA eminent domain language, bipartisan UAP Caucus), the ODNI NDA waiver (July 31, 2026, results pending), and the structural possibility that deeply compartmented programs could exist beyond the reach of AARO's investigation.

**Key uncertainty:** The ODNI NDA waiver represents a genuine test. If individuals released from NDAs produce specific, verifiable, firsthand testimony about materials they personally handled or analyzed -- accompanied by physical evidence subjected to independent laboratory analysis -- this assessment would require significant upward revision. As of the date of this assessment, no such testimony has been publicly produced.

## II. SCOPE AND METHODOLOGY

### Scope

This assessment addresses a single question: **Does the US government (including contractor programs operating under government authority) currently possess physical material samples of verified anomalous or non-human origin?**

This question was identified as the sole area of genuine uncertainty ("Genuinely Uncertain Item #1") in the prior Puthoff/Cataclysm/NEO final assessment, which assigned it a Bayesian posterior of approximately 10.9% -- "unlikely but not dismissable."

The scope explicitly excludes:
- UAP sightings, sensor data, or flight characteristics (assessed separately)
- Biological specimens or medical claims
- The existence of UAP investigation programs (confirmed; not at issue)
- The sincerity of witnesses (assumed for analytic purposes; addressed under counter-hypotheses)

### Methodology

Eight analytical agents conducted parallel investigations across two waves:

**Wave 1 (Evidence Collection and Assessment):**
- M01: Physical material sample provenance and chain of custody (15 samples)
- M02: Government contract trail (AAWSAP, AATIP, CRADA, AARO)
- M03: Independence assessment of all known laboratory analyses
- M04: Defense contractor claims mapping
- M05: Legislative and oversight trail analysis

**Wave 2 (Analytical Integration):**
- M06: Cross-reference of attrition cases versus materials personnel
- M07: Formal network graph construction and analysis
- M08: Counter-hypothesis development and evaluation (5 hypotheses)

Each agent operated independently, with access to prior wave products but not to other agents' concurrent work. All findings are sourced to open-source materials using a four-tier source quality system:

| Tier | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| 1 | Official government record, FOIA release, SEC filing, peer-reviewed publication |
| 2 | Credentialed journalism with named sources and editorial oversight |
| 3 | Conference presentation, named-source interview, domain expert analysis |
| 4 | Social media, anonymous claims, unverified podcast statements |

### Analytic Standards

This assessment follows ICD 203 (Intelligence Community Directive 203) analytic standards. Confidence levels reflect the quality and quantity of available evidence and the strength of analytic reasoning, not certainty of outcome. Probability language follows the standard IC scale:

| Term | Approximate Probability |
|------|------------------------|
| Almost certainly | >95% |
| Highly likely | 80-95% |
| Likely | 55-80% |
| Roughly even | 45-55% |
| Unlikely | 20-45% |
| Highly unlikely | 5-20% |
| Remote possibility | <5% |

## IV. SOURCE INDEPENDENCE AND NETWORK ANALYSIS

### The Core Finding

Agent M07's formal network analysis represents the single most consequential analytical contribution of this investigation. Its findings reframe the entire materials debate by demonstrating that what appears to be a large body of converging testimony is, in structural terms, a single source repeating through institutional cycling.

### Network Metrics

| Metric | Value | Significance |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| Core network density | 0.87 | Extremely high; nearly every member connected to every other member |
| Clustering coefficient | ~1.0 | Complete clustering; no independent sub-clusters |
| Effective independent source count (N_eff) | ~1.0-1.2 | Calculated as N / (1 + (N-1) * rho) where rho > 0.95 |
| Minimum vertex cut | 2 (Puthoff + Elizondo) | Removing two individuals severs ALL evidence paths |
| Number of core Tier 1 nodes | 6-8 | Same individuals appear at virtually every custody/analysis/advocacy node |

### The Independence Paradox

A critical diagnostic pattern emerges from the network analysis:

- **Network-internal analyses** (0-2 degrees of separation): Frequently claim anomalous or suggestive results, but never in peer-reviewed publications with supporting data.
- **Network-independent analyses** (3+ degrees of separation, especially government labs): Uniformly find terrestrial, conventional materials with no anomalous properties.

This pattern -- where claims of anomaly correlate perfectly with network proximity and disappear entirely at network boundaries -- is precisely what would be expected if the claims originate within the network rather than reflecting properties of the materials themselves. It is inconsistent with the hypothesis that genuinely anomalous materials exist but are only recognized by network-connected analysts.

### Institutional Cycling Without Evidentiary Accumulation

Agent M07 identified four temporal phases of institutional cycling:

1. **CIA/SRI Phase** (1972-1995): Puthoff, Green, Davis, Vallee at SRI International under STARGATE
2. **Private Phase** (1995-2008): Same individuals migrate to Bigelow's NIDS
3. **DIA Phase** (2008-2012): Same individuals secure AAWSAP contract through Bigelow's BAASS
4. **Public Phase** (2017-present): Same individuals co-found TTSA, then migrate to Sol Foundation, congressional advisory roles

The individuals cycle through institutions, but the evidence base does not accumulate. Each phase begins by referencing the same materials (Art's Parts, Ubatuba, Wilson-Davis memo) and ends without producing new independently verified physical evidence. The institutional affiliations change; the underlying evidentiary deficit does not.

### Funding Flow

The funding pathway traces a consistent arc: CIA (STARGATE) -> Bigelow private funds (NIDS) -> DIA contract via Bigelow (AAWSAP/BAASS) -> TTSA equity crowdfunding -> Sol Foundation membership revenue -> Congressional advisory positions. At each transition, the same personnel convert institutional credibility from the prior phase into access and funding at the next.

## V. BAYESIAN POSTERIOR UPDATE

### Prior

The Puthoff/Cataclysm/NEO final assessment established a prior posterior of approximately 10.9% for H_materials, based on:
- Congressional testimony under oath (Grusch, secondhand)
- Existence of AATIP/AAWSAP as confirmed government programs
- Schumer-Rounds UAP Disclosure Act (implies legislative belief in potential hidden programs)
- IC IG "credible and urgent" determination (procedural)
- AARO found "no empirical evidence" (counterweight)

That assessment rated this as "unlikely but not dismissable."

### New Evidence: Downward Factors

The following new evidence items, not fully incorporated in the prior assessment, decrease the posterior:

**1. ORNL definitive terrestrial finding (Likelihood ratio ~0.3)**
The most famous claimed UAP material, tested by the most capable independent laboratory available, yielded unambiguously terrestrial results. Under H_materials, we would expect at least some anomalous signature in at least one submitted sample. Under ~H_materials, conventional results are expected. This is the single strongest piece of evidence in the entire investigation.

**2. Complete null results from ALL independent testing (Likelihood ratio ~0.4)**
Agent M03's consolidated independence matrix demonstrates that no analysis at network distance >= 3 has ever found anomalous properties. Every anomalous claim traces to network distance 0-2. This systematic pattern is highly improbable under H_materials (which predicts anomalous properties should be detectable regardless of who operates the instruments) and highly probable under ~H_materials.

**3. Yankee Blue revelation (Likelihood ratio ~0.5)**
The confirmed existence of a USAF program that deliberately fabricated UFO reverse-engineering briefings from the 1980s through 2023, briefing hundreds of military officers into a nonexistent program, provides a documented mechanism for generating sincere, credible witnesses to programs that do not exist. This directly addresses the strongest upward factor in the prior assessment (credible witness testimony) by demonstrating that such testimony can be produced by design.

**4. PURSUE 375+ files null result (Likelihood ratio ~0.7)**
The ODNI's dedicated declassification effort released 375+ files with zero materials-related evidence. Under H_materials, some documentary trace of materials programs should appear in this volume of released records. Under ~H_materials, the null is expected.

**5. N_eff ~1.0 (Likelihood ratio ~0.5)**
The prior assessment treated multiple witnesses as partially independent sources. The network analysis demonstrates that they function as effectively a single source. This does not introduce new evidence but recalibrates the weight of existing testimony.

**6. Nolan interview-publication gap (Likelihood ratio ~0.7)**
A key witness's public claims systematically exceed what his peer-reviewed publications support. Under H_materials, a scientist with evidence of anomalous materials would publish it. Under ~H_materials, the gap between conference claims and publications is expected when the data do not support the stronger claims.

### New Evidence: Upward or Stabilizing Factors

**1. Sustained bipartisan congressional action (Likelihood ratio ~1.3)**
The UAPDA eminent domain language, bipartisan UAP Caucus formation, and Burlison FFRDC demands represent genuine legislative seriousness that goes beyond what network lobbying alone would typically produce. However, Agent M05 found that no legislator has publicly claimed to have personally seen materials or definitive classified evidence, limiting the upward pressure.

**2. ODNI NDA waiver (Likelihood ratio: pending)**
The July 31, 2026 waiver is the most significant pending development. It creates a mechanism by which new, potentially independent testimony could emerge. However, as of this assessment's date, no such testimony has been produced. The waiver's existence is slightly upward (the executive branch considered it worth issuing) but its impact remains unrealized.

**3. Structural concealment possibility (Likelihood ratio ~1.1)**
The SAP/USAP/WUSAP framework provides a documented mechanism by which programs could exist beyond AARO's investigative reach. This is a logical possibility that prevents categorical dismissal but does not constitute positive evidence.

### Posterior Calculation

Starting from the prior of 10.9%, the combined downward factors (items 1-6) produce a substantial reduction. The combined upward factors (items 1-3) provide modest stabilization.

Using sequential Bayesian updating with the likelihood ratios estimated above:

| Step | Factor | Prior | LR | Posterior |
|------|--------|-------|-----|----------|
| 0 | Starting prior | 10.9% | -- | 10.9% |
| 1 | ORNL null result | 10.9% | 0.3 | 3.5% |
| 2 | All independent tests null | 3.5% | 0.4 | 1.4% |
| 3 | Yankee Blue | 1.4% | 0.5 | 0.7% |
| 4 | PURSUE null | 0.7% | 0.7 | 0.5% |
| 5 | N_eff recalibration | 0.5% | 0.5 | 0.3% |
| 6 | Nolan gap | 0.3% | 0.7 | 0.2% |
| 7 | Congressional action | 0.2% | 1.3 | 0.3% |
| 8 | NDA waiver (existence) | 0.3% | 1.0 | 0.3% |
| 9 | Concealment possibility | 0.3% | 1.1 | 0.3% |

**Naive sequential calculation: ~0.3%**

However, this naive calculation overstates precision and suffers from several methodological limitations:

1. **Double-counting:** Several evidence items are not fully independent (e.g., ORNL null and "all independent tests null" overlap substantially; N_eff recalibration reweights rather than introducing new evidence).
2. **Likelihood ratio estimation uncertainty:** Each LR estimate carries substantial uncertainty. The true LR for ORNL, for example, could reasonably range from 0.1 to 0.6 depending on assumptions about how anomalous materials would present under testing.
3. **Model uncertainty:** The sequential Bayesian framework assumes evidence items can be treated as conditionally independent given the hypothesis, which is approximate at best.
4. **Residual structural uncertainty:** The possibility of deeply compartmented programs that have successfully concealed genuinely anomalous materials from every investigation, every declassification effort, and every independent laboratory represents a tail risk that simple likelihood ratios cannot fully capture.

**Correcting for these limitations** -- particularly double-counting, which inflates the downward revision -- and applying appropriate uncertainty bounds:

**Revised posterior: approximately 5-8%**

This aligns with Agent M08's independent estimate and reflects the following reasoning:
- The evidence strongly favors ~H_materials over H_materials
- The downward pressure is substantial but not overwhelming
- A hard floor of approximately 3-5% is imposed by structural concealment uncertainty (SAP/USAP/WUSAP framework, possible programs beyond AARO reach, NDA waiver results pending)
- The assessment cannot go lower than "highly unlikely" without either (a) access to classified compartmented program records or (b) positive evidence that all claimed programs have been identified and are conventional

### Confidence Assessment

**Confidence level: MODERATE**

Factors supporting higher confidence:
- Large volume of open-source evidence examined (8 agents, hundreds of sources)
- Convergent findings across independent analytical lines
- The strongest downward evidence (ORNL, PURSUE, Yankee Blue) comes from Tier 1 sources
- Network analysis methodology is robust and reproducible

Factors limiting confidence:
- The investigation is entirely open-source; classified evidence is unavailable
- The structural concealment hypothesis cannot be tested with open-source methods
- The NDA waiver results are pending and could materially alter the assessment
- The sample of materials submitted for independent testing may not be representative (advocates claim "the real materials are still classified")

## VI. ANALYSIS OF COMPETING HYPOTHESES

Five hypotheses were developed and evaluated by Agent M08 against the complete evidence base. They are treated as potentially overlapping rather than mutually exclusive.

### H1: Institutional Capture / Funding Ecosystem

**Statement:** The $22M AAWSAP contract, Bigelow's private funding, TTSA's business model, and the conference/foundation circuit have created a self-sustaining ecosystem in which extraordinary claims generate funding, attention, and professional rewards, while null results are suppressed or reinterpreted.

**Consistency with evidence:** HIGH. H1 accounts for all six major findings: independent test null results (threaten funding, so downplayed), N_eff ~1.0 (network formed around funding opportunities), EarthTech null (never published; materials continued to be promoted), Nolan interview-publication gap (no penalty for overclaiming), PURSUE null (reframed as "they haven't released the good stuff"), and congressional interest based on secondhand testimony (legislators are downstream consumers of network claims).

**Predictions confirmed:** 6 of 6 specific predictions confirmed, including that financial incentives should track with claim intensity, that null results should be suppressed or reframed, and that new entrants should be financially connected to existing network.

**Parsimony:** HIGH. Requires only documented funding flows and well-understood institutional dynamics.

### H2: Cold War Legacy / Misattribution (Including Yankee Blue)

**Statement:** Classified but terrestrial military and intelligence programs (STARGATE, Project Mogul, Yankee Blue, advanced materials research at Battelle/ORNL/Sandia) were misidentified as exotic technology programs, either by participants who lacked full access or by later investigators encountering fragmentary records.

**Consistency with evidence:** HIGH. H2 accounts for the complete null results (the materials are terrestrial because the programs were terrestrial), the Wilson-Davis memo (Wilson encountered a conventional SAP and was denied access, which was reinterpreted as evidence of a crash retrieval program), and the pattern of "memory metal" and exotic alloy references (NiTi shape-memory alloys were classified research at Battelle in the 1960s).

**Critical discovery -- Yankee Blue:** This USAF program deliberately fabricated UFO reverse-engineering briefings from the 1980s through 2023 and briefed hundreds of military officers under NDAs. It provides a documented, confirmed mechanism for generating exactly the type of sincere, credible witness testimony that forms the strongest evidence for H_materials. Yankee Blue alone is sufficient to explain how multiple apparently independent military witnesses could report firsthand knowledge of crash retrieval programs that do not exist.

**Predictions confirmed:** 5 of 5, including that AARO should identify conventional programs behind witness claims (confirmed), that witnesses should report unusual-but-not-impossible properties (confirmed), and that Yankee Blue-type programs should produce sincere witnesses (confirmed).

**Parsimony:** HIGH. Requires only documented government programs and standard misattribution.

### H3: Sincere Belief Amplification

**Statement:** Core network members (particularly Puthoff, Davis, Green, Vallee) sincerely believe they have encountered evidence of non-human technology based on their decades of work in consciousness/parapsychology research, and this sincere belief drives continued advocacy independent of financial incentives.

**Consistency with evidence:** HIGH. H3 accounts for the sustained commitment of individuals who could have pursued more lucrative or prestigious careers, the escalation of claims over time (consistent with belief perseverance / confirmation bias), and the network's homogeneity of views (shared prior beliefs create shared interpretations).

**Predictions confirmed:** 5 of 5, including that network members should have pre-existing paranormal beliefs (confirmed for all core members), that belief intensity should correlate with network centrality (confirmed), and that new evidence should be interpreted through existing belief framework (confirmed -- every new data point is reinterpreted to support pre-existing conclusions).

**Parsimony:** MODERATE. Requires assumptions about psychological dynamics but these are well-documented in cognitive science literature.

### H4: Strategic Deception / Disinformation

**Statement:** Elements of the US national security apparatus have deliberately promoted or permitted UFO/UAP narratives as cover for classified conventional programs, as strategic deception against adversaries, or as counterintelligence tools.

**Consistency with evidence:** MODERATE-HIGH. H4 is directly confirmed by Yankee Blue. The historical pattern (Project Mogul/Roswell cover story, CIA encouragement of UFO explanations for U-2 sightings) establishes a documented precedent. The AARO Historical Record Report acknowledged that some UAP reports were cover for sensitive programs.

**Predictions confirmed:** 3 of 3 fully, plus one partially confirmed. The same pattern recurs across decades (confirmed: Mogul 1947, U-2/Blue Book 1950s-60s, Yankee Blue 1980s-2023, current claims all follow the same template).

**Parsimony:** MODERATE. Requires an intentional actor but documented examples exist.

### H5: Residual Genuine Anomaly

**Statement:** Despite the failure of all tested samples and the demonstrated sufficiency of H1-H4, there remains a non-zero probability that genuine anomalous materials exist in deeply compartmented programs that have not been penetrated by any investigation to date.

**Consistency with evidence:** LOW-MODERATE. H5 is consistent with the evidence only in the trivial sense that absence of evidence is not evidence of absence. It cannot explain why every independent test fails, why the network's own laboratory (EarthTech) found nothing anomalous, or why PURSUE and AARO found no evidence. Its consistency depends entirely on the unfalsifiable claim that "the real evidence is still hidden."

**Predictions:** H5 makes specific predictions that have consistently failed:
- The NDA waiver should produce dramatic testimony: PENDING (no results yet)
- Independent testing should eventually find anomalies: FAILED (every test to date is null)
- More investigation should produce more evidence: FAILED (AARO, PURSUE, and this investigation all found less evidence, not more)
- At least one sample should show non-terrestrial isotopic ratios: FAILED (ORNL definitively terrestrial)

**Parsimony:** LOW. Requires the most assumptions: that genuinely anomalous materials exist, that they have been successfully concealed from every investigation, that every submitted sample is either not representative or was switched, and that the concealment mechanism has operated perfectly across 75+ years and multiple administrations.

**Structural unfalsifiability:** In its strong form ("the real materials exist but are too classified to verify"), H5 cannot be falsified by any open-source investigation. This is a feature of the hypothesis, not evidence in its favor. Analytically, unfalsifiable hypotheses receive weight only to the extent that their prior probability and known concealment mechanisms justify -- which, given the documented SAP/USAP/WUSAP framework, is non-trivial but small.

### Combined Assessment

H1 + H2 + H3 + H4, operating in combination, fully accounts for all observed evidence. H5 adds explanatory power only for evidence that does not exist (the hypothetical anomalous materials themselves). The parsimony advantage of the combined H1-H4 explanation over any model incorporating H5 is substantial.

## VII. KEY UNCERTAINTIES AND COLLECTION GAPS

### Uncertainties That Could Shift the Assessment Upward

1. **ODNI NDA Waiver Results (HIGH potential impact)**
   The July 31, 2026 waiver releases individuals from non-disclosure agreements for purposes of PURSUE Task Force testimony. If this produces firsthand, specific, verifiable testimony from individuals who personally handled or analyzed anomalous materials -- especially if accompanied by physical evidence submitted to independent laboratories -- it would require significant upward revision. Estimated potential impact: could raise posterior to 15-30% depending on specificity and verifiability of testimony.

2. **Classified Compartmented Program Records (MODERATE-HIGH potential impact)**
   This investigation is entirely open-source. If SAP/USAP/WUSAP records exist that document genuine anomalous materials recovery, they are beyond the reach of this analysis. The Schumer-Rounds UAPDA was designed to create a legal mechanism for reviewing such records. If the UAP Records Review Board (proposed under UAPDA) gains access to currently compartmented records and identifies evidence of anomalous materials programs, it would require fundamental reassessment.

3. **New Independent Laboratory Analysis (HIGH potential impact)**
   If a material sample demonstrating non-terrestrial isotopic ratios, impossible-by-known-physics properties, or manufacturing techniques beyond current capability is independently verified by two or more laboratories at network distance >= 3, this assessment would require major upward revision. No such analysis has occurred to date.

### Uncertainties That Could Shift the Assessment Further Downward

4. **NDA Waiver Produces Null Results (MODERATE impact)**
   If released witnesses either decline to testify, provide only secondhand accounts, or produce testimony that is contradicted by documentary evidence, the posterior would decrease to approximately 3-4%. This would effectively close the window of uncertainty that currently prevents categorical dismissal.

5. **Additional Yankee Blue-Type Program Disclosures (MODERATE impact)**
   If additional fabricated briefing programs are identified, further eroding the credibility of witness testimony, the posterior could decrease to 2-3%.

6. **Network Analysis of New Claimants (LOW-MODERATE impact)**
   If individuals presented as "new, independent" witnesses (e.g., the unnamed House Caucus whistleblower, Barber) are demonstrated to be embedded in the existing network at 0-2 degrees of separation, the N_eff estimate would be further confirmed and the posterior would decrease marginally.

### Collection Gaps

- **No access to classified program records** (by design -- this is an open-source investigation)
- **No access to ICIG investigative findings** beyond the procedural "credible and urgent" determination
- **No access to classified congressional briefing content** (legislators who have received classified briefings have not disclosed their content)
- **No physical samples available for independent analysis** by this investigation
- **NDA waiver testimony not yet available** as of assessment date
- **Yankee Blue program documentation** not fully declassified; scope and participant list not publicly available

## IX. DISSENT AND ALTERNATIVE VIEWS

### Registered Dissents

No formal dissent is registered. All eight analytical agents converge on the direction of the assessment (downward revision from the prior 10.9%). Variance exists in the magnitude of revision:

- Agent M08 estimates 5-8% (adopted as the integrated estimate)
- The naive sequential Bayesian calculation yields ~0.3% (rejected as methodologically flawed due to double-counting and false precision)
- A reasonable lower bound, accounting for structural concealment uncertainty, is approximately 3%
- A reasonable upper bound, weighting congressional action more heavily, is approximately 10%

The adopted range of 5-8% reflects the central tendency of these estimates after correcting for known biases.

### Alternative Views Considered

**Alternative View A: "Congressional action constitutes stronger evidence than assessed."**

Proponents of this view argue that the UAPDA eminent domain language, bipartisan support, and sustained legislative attention would not persist if legislators had not seen compelling classified evidence. This view assigns a likelihood ratio of 2.0-3.0 to congressional action rather than the 1.3 used in this assessment.

**Assessment:** This view is weakened by Agent M05's finding that no legislator has publicly stated they have personally seen materials or definitive classified evidence. What legislators report is witness testimony and bureaucratic obstruction -- not physical evidence. The UAPDA can be explained as a response to credible-seeming testimony (H3) combined with genuine institutional opacity, rather than as evidence of actual materials. However, this alternative view cannot be excluded, and it would raise the posterior to approximately 10-12%, roughly returning to the prior.

**Alternative View B: "The assessment should go lower because H1-H4 are sufficient and H5 is unfalsifiable."**

Proponents argue that the hard floor imposed by structural concealment uncertainty is unjustified. If H1-H4 fully account for all evidence, the residual probability should be driven toward the base rate for any extraordinary claim without supporting evidence (approximately 1-2%).

**Assessment:** This view has analytical merit but fails to account for the genuine uniqueness of the congressional and executive branch response. While H1-H4 explain the evidence, they do not fully explain why serious legislators continue to invest political capital in UAP oversight. The structural concealment mechanism (SAP/USAP/WUSAP) is documented and real, even if no evidence confirms it has been used for materials programs. A floor of approximately 3-5% is analytically justified.
