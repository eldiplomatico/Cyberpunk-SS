# M07: Access Network Analysis for Claimed UAP Materials Programs

## Formal Network Mapping of Individuals, Institutions, and Evidence Chains

**Agent:** M07 -- Network Analysis
**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Classification:** OPEN SOURCE
**Methodology:** Social network analysis applied to individuals and institutions named across Wave 1 materials reports (M01-M05), the Puthoff investigation (Agents 01-13), and supplementary web search for 2024-2026 developments. Every node and edge is sourced to the underlying reports.

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This report constructs a formal network graph of every individual and institution named in custody, analysis, advocacy, or oversight roles for claimed UAP materials. The central finding is that the network is extraordinarily dense, dominated by a single tightly-interconnected cluster of 6-8 core individuals who appear at virtually every node in the evidence chain. The effective independent source count (N_eff) remains approximately 1.0-1.2 for materials claims specifically. No claimed evidence path reaches congressional oversight without passing through at least one core network member. The minimum vertex cut -- the smallest number of individuals whose removal would sever all evidence chains from alleged source to Congress -- is TWO (Puthoff and Elizondo). Newly emerged claimants in 2024-2026 (Barber, the unnamed House Caucus whistleblower) have not yet been demonstrated to be independent of the network, and several (Gallaudet, Shellenberger's anonymous source) are directly embedded in it.

## 4. NETWORK METRICS (QUALITATIVE)

### 4.1 Network Density

The core network (Puthoff, Davis, Green, Bigelow, Vallee, Kelleher) exhibits near-complete density. Of the 15 possible pairwise connections among 6 individuals, at least 13 are documented (density = 0.87). ### 4.2 Clustering Coefficient

The clustering coefficient for the core network approaches 1.0 -- almost every pair of individuals who share a connection with a third individual also share a connection with each other. This is the signature of a clique, not a distributed network.

The extended network shows high clustering around three institutional hubs:

These three clusters overlap through the persistent presence of Puthoff (in all three), Mellon (in TTSA and Sol Foundation), and Vallee (in NIDS/BAASS and Sol Foundation).

**Primary bottleneck: Harold Puthoff** -- The only individual present in all three major institutional clusters spanning 50+ years. He connects the Cold War intelligence community (SRI/CIA), the Bigelow-funded era (NIDS/BAASS), the public advocacy era (TTSA), and the current academic-policy era (Sol Foundation). **Tertiary bottleneck: Christopher Mellon** -- Bridges the intelligence community (DASD, SSCI staff) and the advocacy organizations (TTSA, Sol Foundation, Galileo Project). His former government position provides credibility markers that other network members lack.

## 10. CRITICAL FINDINGS

### Finding 1: Effective Source Count for Materials Claims Remains ~1.0

Despite the appearance of multiple independent sources, the materials evidence chain traces back to the same interconnected group. The inter-source correlation (rho) exceeds 0.95 for the core network, yielding N_eff = N / (1 + (N-1) * rho) approximately equal to 1.0-1.2 for N=6, rho=0.95. What appears to be convergent testimony from multiple credentialed individuals is, analytically, a single data point repeated through different institutional contexts.

### Finding 2: Every Independent Analysis Found Terrestrial Results

The pattern is absolute and exceptionless:
- Network-internal analyses: claim anomalous properties (unpublished or interview-only) OR find nothing anomalous (Puthoff's own 2012 admission)
- Independent analyses (ORNL, Reiter, Carnegie, Brazilian CTA, Powell et al.): ALL find terrestrial composition

This is the exact pattern predicted by a null hypothesis where no anomalous materials exist.

### Finding 3: Institutional Cycling Without Evidentiary Accumulation

The network has cycled through five institutional wrappers over 50 years (SRI -> NIDS -> BAASS -> TTSA -> Sol Foundation) without any accumulation of independently verifiable evidence. Each new institution adds credibility markers (government contract, SEC filing, Stanford affiliation, congressional testimony) but not independent physical evidence. The materials themselves -- principally Art's Parts -- have circulated through the network since 1996 without any new anomalous sample entering the system.

### Finding 4: The Sol Foundation Represents Network Convergence, Not Independence

The Sol Foundation (est. 2023) brings together individuals from multiple previous network nodes into a single organization:
- Nolan (Stanford, CIA brain imaging via Green)
- Grusch (NRO, AARO, whistleblower)
- Mellon (DoD, SSCI, TTSA)
- Gallaudet (NOAA, Navy)
- With Puthoff and Vallee as featured speakers

This is presented as an independent academic think tank but is functionally a convergence node for the existing network. Its co-founders' appointment to the government UAP Science Advisory Council (June 2026) represents the network achieving its most direct access to policy-making to date.

### Finding 5: The NDA Waiver Is the First True Test of Independent Source Existence

The ODNI NDA waiver (July 31, 2026) removes the primary barrier cited for why independent contractor employees have not come forward. If the waiver fails to produce publicly identified independent sources over a period of months, this would constitute significant evidence against the existence of the alleged programs. Conversely, if genuinely independent contractor employees come forward with verifiable information, this would be the first evidence to break the network's effective monopoly on materials claims. As of September 3, 2026, five weeks after the waiver, no such disclosure has been publicly identified.

### Finding 6: Observation Witnesses Are Independent; Materials Claimants Are Not

A meaningful distinction exists between two categories of witnesses:
- **Observation witnesses** (Fravor, Graves, Nuccetelli, Borland, Wiggins): These individuals describe their own direct sensory experiences with anomalous phenomena. Their testimony is genuinely firsthand and several appear independent of the core network. However, NONE of them make materials claims.
- **Materials claimants** (Puthoff, Davis, Bigelow, Elizondo, Grusch): These individuals claim knowledge of recovered craft and materials. ALL are embedded in the network, and ALL rely on secondhand information (even Puthoff acknowledges he has "not had direct access to the materials").

The observation witness category and the materials claimant category do not overlap. No individual who has directly observed an anomalous phenomenon has also claimed to have handled or examined recovered materials.
